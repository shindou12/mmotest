# v3で使った画像生成プロンプト

組み込み image_gen を使用。APIキー、従量課金API、追加インストールは使用していない。画像はassets/にコピーし、元画像の画素を加工せず、実行時にUVで部品を切り出す。

## chair

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: orthographic component texture atlas for an old Japanese school chair, assembled from separate 2D image planes in a game. Generate ONE transparent PNG asset sheet laid out in a strict 2 by 2 grid of equal square cells, with clear transparent gutters. Neutral evenly lit material photography, no cast shadows or background, no perspective. Upper-left cell: ONLY a square school-chair seat seen EXACTLY straight down, warm worn brown laminated plywood, gently rounded square corners, four small recessed fasteners, aged edge, thin fine scratches, entire seat centered inside its cell with 12 percent transparent padding. Upper-right cell: ONLY an elongated horizontal rectangular curved plywood school-chair backrest seen EXACTLY straight from the front, same warm brown wood and age, two visible pairs of metal fasteners, rounded corners, no legs no supports; width about 80 percent of cell, height about 38 percent, centered. Lower-left cell: ONLY a single long vertical narrow gray painted steel tube seen EXACTLY perpendicular to its side, full length straight up/down, width 8 percent of cell and height 84 percent of cell, chipped dark gray paint and subtle blue steel reflections; square ends, no bends, no wood; centered. Lower-right cell: ONLY underside view of the same square plywood seat as upper left, same outer contour and size, brown dusty backing with screws, no rails or legs. No assembled chair, no 3D perspective views, no labels, no lettering, no decorative grid lines. True transparent alpha outside each component. Crisp clean silhouettes, detailed restrained surface textures, physically consistent scale within the wood parts.

## back

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a flat back-wall texture for a dark old Japanese classroom in a real time horror game. A perfectly straight-on orthographic photograph of a single RECTANGULAR WALL ONLY, landscape 4:3, no room perspective, no floor, no ceiling, no side walls, no furniture, no people, no windows. Old pale desaturated sage plaster wall with a dusty wide dark green chalkboard across the middle, narrow dark wood chalk tray, a small cream analog wall clock centered above the board, small faded timetable paper fixed at far right, beige darker lower wall band, scuffed dark thin skirting at bottom. Board is blank except faint erased chalk smudges. Entire wall rectangle fills the image edge to edge. Even soft overcast illumination without directional cast shadows, no dramatic lighting, no black vignetting, no glowing light. Realistic worn textures, atmosphere through material aging rather than lighting. No readable words. Designed to receive runtime blue moonlight and warm corridor light.

## desk

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: alpha-cutout school desk furniture card, frontal orthographic. One old Japanese school pupil desk ONLY, viewed from exactly the front at seat height, orthographic no perspective, horizontal top surface visible only as a narrow thin edge. Small rectangular worn honey-brown laminate desktop, gray painted steel tubular frame and four thin legs, open space underneath, small metal shelf beneath top. Feet all aligned on one perfectly horizontal baseline. A matching plywood school chair tucked behind it, only top of backrest and parts of thin legs visible. Entire object isolated on TRUE TRANSPARENT background, no ground plane, no drop shadows, no reflections, no room, no labels. Neutral even soft lighting, dark aged steel but readable details, consistent realistic material scale. Full desk in frame, 8 percent transparent margin. Desktop width to floor-to-desktop height ratio about 0.9. No wide-angle distortion, no stylization. Cutout holes between legs must be transparent.
